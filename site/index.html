Concept

What is H2A2H?

H2A2H separates semantic intent, delegated authority, transport declaration, execution authorization and Human-return proof. An Agent can participate without hiding transport, authorization or responsibility logic inside domain behavior.

Protocol composition

Independent layers, one verifiable interaction

Each concern remains explicit and composable. The H2A2H envelope binds correlation, causation, identity, delegation and responsibility references across the chain.

Design principles

Semantics before hidden behavior

  • 01
    Semantic identityActions and capabilities share stable canonical_label identities.
  • 02
    Explicit delegationAuthority is bounded, inspectable and revocable.
  • 03
    Transport independenceCommunication channels are declared, not buried in domain code.
  • 04
    Auditable outcomesReceipts, traces and provenance preserve the execution story.
  • 05
    Human accountabilityConsequential delegation ends at a verifiable Human boundary.
